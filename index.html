Model and data boundary

The built-in browser mode is a reduced vertical point-mass demonstration. Imported mode consumes the exact aerocps.telemetry.v1 CSV contract emitted by AeroCPSSimulation's vertical-flight executable. It does not convert that contract into six-degree-of-freedom data or imply certified aircraft behavior.
